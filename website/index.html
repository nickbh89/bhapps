Small apps that stop drivers getting caught out.

BH Apps builds Android apps for UK drivers around one idea: detect the charge, remind you before the deadline, and keep your location on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no data sold.

Toll Mate

Toll roads & charging zones

Detects barrierless toll crossings, clean air zones and airport drop-off charges you have to pay online afterwards, and reminds you before the deadline passes — with a link straight to the operator's payment page.

ParkMate

Private car parks

Notices when you've pulled into a privately enforced car park and tells you when you need to leave, before a Parking Charge Notice lands on your doormat. Thousands of UK sites, refreshed weekly.

Who's behind it

BH Apps is currently a team of one, building Android apps for UK drivers. Each app started with a charge that was easy to miss and expensive to forget — and a belief that a reminder is only useful if you can trust it.

The apps are free. If one of them saves you a penalty, you can leave a tip.

  • Only the charges you can forgetPay-at-barrier sites never trigger an alert. False alarms teach people to ignore the real ones.
  • Location stays on the deviceGeofencing runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to create.
  • Signs are the final wordRules and prices change. The apps are a safety net, and they say so.
  • Updates without an updateBoundaries, prices and car-park data sync from a public source, so fixes reach you without a new release.
